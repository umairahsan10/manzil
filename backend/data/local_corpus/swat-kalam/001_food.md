# Kalam, Swat — Local Food & Dining

Kalam is the main hub of upper Swat Valley, and its food scene reflects the region's Pashtun and Kohistani heritage.

**Trout** is the culinary highlight. The Ushu River and its tributaries are stocked with brown trout, and several restaurants along the river serve fresh-caught fish. **Kalam Hotel & Restaurant** has the most reliable trout preparation — simple grilled with lemon and local herbs (PKR 1,200–1,800).

**Lamb karahi** in Swat has a distinct style — heavier on dried tomatoes and green chillies than Punjab or GB versions. **Green Hills Restaurant** near the main bazaar is a local favourite for this dish.

**Mahodand Lake** (day-trip from Kalam) has no restaurants — bring a packed lunch from Kalam. Some jeep drivers arrange basic tea and biscuits at the lake.

**Practical notes:** Kalam is cheaper than Hunza or Skardu for dining. A full meal for two at a mid-range restaurant runs PKR 1,500–2,500. The town has basic grocery stores for snack supplies. Water is from mountain springs and generally safe.
