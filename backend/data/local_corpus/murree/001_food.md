# Murree — Local Food & Dining

Murree's food scene is defined by hill-station comfort food rather than regional cuisine. It is the most developed dining destination in this dataset, with options spanning street snacks to full-service restaurants.

**Mall Road** is the culinary epicentre. The stretch from Pindi Point to Kashmir Point has dozens of restaurants, cafes, and street-food vendors. **Saffron Restaurant** (near GPO Chowk) is the most consistently rated for Pakistani and continental dishes. Their karahi and grilled trout are reliable; expect PKR 1,500–2,500 per person.

**Street food:** Murree is famous for its **roasted corn** (bhutta) — vendors line Mall Road from late afternoon, rubbing charred corn with lemon and masala. **Kashmiri chai** (pink, salty tea with nuts) is available at every tea stall and is the definitive Murree beverage. **Gol gappay** and **dahi bhallay** carts are clustered near the Chair Lift area.

**Patriata** (New Murree, 15 km from the main town) has fewer dining options but **Green Valley Restaurant** offers decent desi food with a view. The chair-lift area has basic snack stalls — samosas, pakoras, and instant noodles for cold-weather comfort.

**Breakfast:** Most hotels include a standard Pakistani breakfast. **Hotel Move-N-Pick's** breakfast buffet is the most extensive in town if you are not staying there (PKR 1,200 per person for non-guests).

**Practical notes:** Murree is the most expensive destination in this dataset for dining. Mall Road restaurants charge a premium for the location. A meal for two at a mid-range restaurant easily reaches PKR 4,000–5,000. Hygiene is generally better than remote northern areas due to higher turnover. Water is safe — Murree has municipal supply.
