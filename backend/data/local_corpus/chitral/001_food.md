# Chitral — Local Food & Dining

Chitral town has a small but distinct food culture influenced by Central Asian trade routes and the Kalash valleys.

**Chitrali pulao** is the local speciality — rice cooked with dried fruits, carrots, and mild spices, closer to Afghan/Uzbek styles than Pakistani pulao. **Chitral View Hotel** serves the most reliable version in town.

**Chapli kebab** in Chitral has a unique local style — flatter and spicier than Peshawari versions, with more coriander and pomegranate seeds. **Pakistan Hotel** near the bus stand is the local favourite for this.

**Kalash Valleys** (Bumburet, Rumbur, Birir) offer a rare chance to try **Kalash cuisine** — distinct from mainstream Pakistani food. Walnut bread, grape wine (locally made, low alcohol), and goat cheese are specialities. These are only available in home-stays during festival season (May, August, December).

**Practical notes:** Chitral is isolated and supplies are limited. Fresh produce is seasonal. Prices are moderate — PKR 800–1,500 per meal. English menus are rare outside the main hotels.
