# Passu — Local Food & Dining

Passu is a tiny village north of Hunza with extremely limited dining options. Most travellers eat at their guesthouse or day-trip from Karimabad.

**Passu Glacier View Hotel** has the only proper restaurant in the village. They serve basic Pakistani fare — daal, roti, chicken curry, and the occasional mutton dish. The quality is acceptable but not remarkable. The view of the Passu Cathedral peaks from the dining terrace is the real draw.

**Hussaini Village** (10 minutes walk from Passu) has a few home-stay style setups where local families cook traditional Wakhi and Burushaski dishes. These are not advertised online — ask your guesthouse host to arrange a meal. Expect simple fare: chapshuro, local daal, and fresh bread.

**Practical notes:** Most travellers staying in Passu book half-board or full-board at their guesthouse. Do not expect standalone restaurants. Bring snacks from Hunza. Water is from mountain springs and generally safe.
