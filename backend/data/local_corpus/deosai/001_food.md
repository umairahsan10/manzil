# Deosai Plains — Local Food & Dining

Deosai has no restaurants, no shops, and no permanent human settlement. This is wilderness dining — you bring everything you need.

**Most visitors travel with a guide/cook from Skardu** who prepares meals at camp. The standard menu is simple: tinned food, rice, daal, and instant noodles. Some operators bring fresh vegetables and meat in coolers.

**Self-catering:** If you are visiting independently (not recommended for first-timers), you must carry all food from Skardu. There are no water purification issues — streams are glacier-fed and pristine — but you need a stove and fuel.

**Practical notes:** Deosai is not a food destination. Plan for survival calories, not culinary experiences. Bring high-energy snacks — nuts, chocolate, dried fruit. Altitude suppresses appetite, so do not over-pack.
