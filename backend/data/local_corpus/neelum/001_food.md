# Neelum Valley — Local Food & Dining

Neelum Valley's food scene is basic but offers a few local specialities.

**Kashmiri cuisine** dominates. The signature dish is **Kashmiri pulao** — rice cooked with dried fruits, nuts, and mild spices. **Rogan josh** (lamb in red curry) is also common. **Sharda Restaurant** near the Sharda archaeological site is the most reliable spot for these dishes.

**Trout** is available in the upper valley near Kel and Taobat. The Neelum River has wild trout, though stocks are smaller than Swat. **PTDC Kel** serves trout when available.

**Practical notes:** Neelum Valley is less developed for tourism than Hunza or Swat. Dining options are limited to basic dhabas and hotel restaurants. Bring snacks from Muzaffarabad. Prices are moderate — PKR 800–1,500 per meal.
