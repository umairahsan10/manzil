# Attabad Lake — Local Food & Dining

Attabad Lake is primarily a sightseeing stop rather than a dining destination. The lakeside has a handful of basic eateries.

**Lake View Restaurant** near the tunnel entrance serves fish curry, chicken karahi, and daal-roti. The fish is claimed to be from the lake itself (trout and local carp), though supply is inconsistent. Prices are inflated due to the tourist location — PKR 1,000–1,800 per meal.

**Boat operators** often serve tea and biscuits on the boat rides. Some larger boats have basic snack service.

**Practical notes:** Most travellers eat at Attabad as a lunch stop between Hunza and Passu/Khunjerab. Do not plan a dedicated dining trip here. The better option is to eat in Karimabad or Gulmit before/after your lake visit.
