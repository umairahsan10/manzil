# Khaplu — Local Food & Dining

Khaplu is a historic town with a small but growing food scene centred around heritage tourism.

**Khaplu Palace Serena** is the standout dining experience. The palace restaurant serves refined Balti cuisine in a restored 19th-century royal residence. The apricot chicken and local trout are highlights. Day visitors can dine with advance notice. Prices are high for the region (PKR 2,000–3,500) but the setting is unmatched.

**Local dhabas** near the main bazaar serve standard Pakistani fare. **Al-Mehran Hotel** is the most reliable of the budget options — decent karahi and fresh roti for PKR 500–900.

**Practical notes:** Khaplu is less touristed than Skardu, so English menus are rare. Pointing at what other diners are eating is a valid strategy. Stock up on snacks in Skardu before the 2-hour drive to Khaplu.
