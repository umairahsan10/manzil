# Gilgit — Local Food & Dining

Gilgit is the regional capital and transit hub, so its food scene is more varied than smaller towns but less charming than Hunza or Skardu.

**Ustad BBQ** near the bus stand is the most famous local spot. Their beef tikka and seekh kebabs are grilled over charcoal and served with fresh tandoori roti. A full meal costs PKR 600–1,000. It is a no-frills, high-turnover place — do not expect ambience.

**Hunza Lounge** (also near the bus stand) serves the best chapshuro in Gilgit — the Hunza-style meat-filled flatbread. It is a good place to try the dish without travelling all the way to Karimabad.

**Gilgit Serena Hotel** has the most refined dining in town. The restaurant serves continental and Pakistani cuisine in a pleasant garden setting. Non-guests are welcome for dinner. Prices are higher (PKR 2,000–3,500 per person) but the consistency and hygiene justify it.

**Practical notes:** As a transit hub, Gilgit has the best grocery stores in the region. Stock up on snacks, water, and supplies here before heading to more remote destinations. The local bazaar sells dried apricots, walnuts, and handmade shawls at reasonable prices.
