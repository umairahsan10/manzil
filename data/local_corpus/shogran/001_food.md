# Shogran — Local Food & Dining

Shogran is a small hill-station with limited but functional dining options.

**Most hotels offer full-board packages** because standalone restaurants are scarce. The food is standard Pakistani tourist fare — chicken karahi, daal, mixed vegetables, and roti. Quality varies by hotel; **Shogran Valley Resort** and **Pine Park Lodges** have the best reputations for food.

**Siri Paye** (the meadow above Shogran) has no dining options beyond a few tea stalls. Bring packed lunch if you plan to spend the day there.

**Practical notes:** Shogran is a 2-hour drive from Balakot, which has better dining and shopping. Stock up on snacks in Balakot or Naran before ascending. Prices at Shogran hotels are inflated due to transport costs.
